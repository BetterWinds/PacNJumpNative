Pac'N-Jump for Dreamcast

The 2011 game was pulled from the stores. This is a rebuilt version for the Sega Dreamcast. It ships with none of the original art or sound: you give this page your own copy of the original Android APK and it hands back one disc image with the game and your data on it. One image, one disc, nothing to import on the console.

Everything happens in your browser. Your APK is not uploaded anywhere.

Tested in Flycast and on a real console (progressive and interlaced video on CRTs), and scores save to the VMU. Burned discs and PAL consoles have not been tried yet.

Drop your original Pac'N-Jump APK here or click to choose the file
    Download PacNJump.cdi

    Using the image

    1. Flycast: open PacNJump.cdi as a game.
    2. Disc emulator on a real console (GDEMU, USB-GDROM and similar): copy it to the card or drive the way you would any other CDI.
    3. A real disc: the image is a self-booting Audio/Data CDI (MIL-CD style). Burn it with software that writes DiscJuggler images, at a low speed.

    It takes a minute or two on a computer, and longer on a phone. The disc is built from a ready-made image that has the game program on it, so the page needs that image to be available (it is added to this site by the Dreamcast build). The source for the game and this page is in the repository. This is a fan project and is not affiliated with Namco, Bandai Namco or Sega.
